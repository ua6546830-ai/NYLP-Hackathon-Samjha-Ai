# Frequently Asked Questions — Samjha? Rx

### 1. Does Samjha? Rx use AI to decide if my medicines are safe?
**No.** Google's Gemini API assists only with transcription and language translation. Clinical safety rules (food requirements, mineral chelation, liver/kidney cautions, daily limits) are evaluated by a **deterministic, versioned rules engine** curated and reviewed by licensed clinical pharmacists using the British National Formulary (BNF) and Pakistan National Formulary (PNF).

### 2. Why does the app lock my schedule sometimes?
If a critical detail like the strength (e.g. 250mg vs 500mg) or dosing frequency is illegible or missing, Samjha? Rx adheres to strict medical ethics: **Never guess missing clinical information.** The schedule remains locked until you or a pharmacist confirms the missing detail.

### 3. Which languages are supported?
Samjha? Rx supports:
1. **English**
2. **Roman Urdu** (everyday phonetic Urdu in Latin alphabet, standard in Pakistan)
3. **Urdu Script (اردو)** (native Right-to-Left Arabic-derived script)

### 4. Is my medical data sent to cloud servers?
No. Saved prescriptions and your 4-digit PIN lock are stored exclusively on your device within the app's secure private sandbox. When sharing a referral note with a pharmacist, personal identifiers (names, addresses, phone numbers) are masked.
