### Headline
**New AI-Powered Platform "Samjha? Rx" Eliminates Prescription Confusion for Bilingual Patients and Delivers Real-Time Clinical Verification for Pharmacists**

### The Story
Every day, millions of patients leave clinics clutching paper prescriptions covered in hurried medical shorthand (`Tab. Amoxyclav 625 mg TDS × 5/7 PC`). When they get home, questions turn into dangerous confusion: *Did the doctor say two or three times a day? Before food or after food? What if I take my heartburn syrup at the same time?*

Studies show that over 60% of outpatient medication errors occur due to misread handwriting, misunderstood Latin abbreviations, and language barriers. Meanwhile, general AI chatbots frequently guess illegible handwriting or hallucinate dosing rules without clinical evidence.

**Samjha? Rx (سمجھا؟)** changes this paradigm. Developed by **Uzair Ahmad**, a final-year Doctor of Pharmacy (Pharm.D) student at FUUAST Karachi with clinical hospital pharmacy experience, the platform translates complex prescription handwriting into clear, everyday instructions across **English, Roman Urdu, and native Urdu script**.

### Key Innovations
1. **The Uncertainty Gate (Never Guess):** Unlike conversational chatbots that hallucinate missing values, Samjha? Rx's multimodal OCR highlights any token with `< 85%` confidence and refuses to unlock dosage schedules until verified by human-in-the-loop confirmation.
2. **Deterministic Clinical Rules Engine:** Safety evaluations are computed using peer-reviewed rules derived from the **British National Formulary (BNF 84)**, **Pakistan National Formulary (PNF)**, and **FDA Drug Safety Monographs**—cross-checking food requirements, fluoroquinolone chelation, and liver toxicity limits.
3. **Dual-Mode Patient & Pharmacist Route:** Seamlessly bridges the patient's daily 24-hour visual schedule with a structured review dashboard for dispensing pharmacists, including an automated **SBAR Prescriber Query Builder**.
4. **On-Device Privacy & Security:** Employs an on-device Room Database (`samjha_rx.db`) explicitly excluded from cloud backups, a 4-digit local screen lock, and zero third-party advertising tracking.
