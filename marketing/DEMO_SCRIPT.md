# 2-Minute Demo Script — Samjha? Rx

**Speaker:** Uzair Ahmad (Pharm.D Candidate) / Presenter

### 00:00 - The Problem
*"Millions of patients leave clinics and pharmacies every day holding a paper prescription. They get home and wonder: Did the doctor say twice a day or three times? Before food or after food? What happens if I take my heartburn syrup at the same time?*

*Today, patients either guess or search online forums that give conflicting advice. We built **Samjha? Rx** to make your prescription make sense."*

### 00:35 - Field-by-Field Human Confirmation & Multilingual Support
*"Watch how Samjha? Rx works on Android.
First, we select a prescription — for example, Augmentin 625mg and Brufen 400mg.
Notice we don't just dump unverified AI output. We present a **Field-by-Field Human Confirmation Gate**. Every brand, strength, and frequency must be visually confirmed.
With one tap, we switch to **Roman Urdu** — 'Khaney ke baad lein' — or native **Urdu script**, making directions accessible to every Pakistani household."*

### 01:10 - Deterministic Safety & The Uncertainty Gate
*"Next, notice our clinical rules engine. We never let an AI model guess clinical safety. Instead, deterministic, pharmacist-reviewed rules flag that Brufen requires stomach protection, and Augmentin must be completed for the full 5-day course.
If a prescription has smudged handwriting with a missing strength (like Scenario 4), the app **refuses to guess**. It locks the daily schedule and prompts: 'Unable to check — Consult a pharmacist'."*

### 01:40 - The Pharmacist Bridge & Privacy Lock
*"Finally, with 'Ask a Pharmacist', patients generate a masked clinical referral note they can share via WhatsApp or in person. And on the Pharmacist Review tab, licensed professionals can audit rule citations and log counseling sign-offs.
All records are saved locally with a PIN lock, ensuring zero external ad tracking.
**Samjha? Rx: Clarity you can read. Safety you can trust.**"*
