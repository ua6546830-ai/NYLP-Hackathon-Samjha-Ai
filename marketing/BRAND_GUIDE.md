# Brand Guide — Samjha? Rx (سمجھا؟)

## 1. Brand Essence
- **Name:** Samjha? Rx (سمجھا؟)
- **Tagline:** Make your prescription make sense. (نسخہ سمجھیں، بے فکری سے صحت مند رہیں)
- **Voice:** Calm, trustworthy, plain-language, clinical without jargon, respectful of patient anxieties and cultural context.

## 2. Color Palette
- **Primary Violet:** `#4F46E5` (Blue-violet accent representing clinical confidence)
- **Misty White:** `#F8FAFC` (Editorial soft background, low glare)
- **Accent Teal:** `#0D9488` (Reassurance and holistic health)
- **Warning Amber:** `#D97706` (Gentle clinical caution / food timing guidance)
- **Critical Rose:** `#E11D48` (Uncertainty flag / missing parameter warning)
- **Clinical Slate:** `#0F172A` & `#1E293B` (High legibility typography)

## 3. Typography
- **English / Roman Urdu:** Clean geometric sans-serif with accessible body sizes (14-16sp).
- **Urdu Script (اردو):** Native Arabic/Urdu RTL typography with generous line heights (24-32sp) for effortless readability.
